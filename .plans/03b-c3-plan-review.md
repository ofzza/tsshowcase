# C3 plan review, round 2 (after the D89 redesign): fresh-eyes triage

A second clean-context agent reviewed everything (REQUIREMENT, AGENTS D1–D89, super-plan R2, C1, C2 with B1, C3 round 5, the earlier triages), focusing on the D89 isolation model. It verified claims on the web (fragment navigation doesn't reach shadow trees; outer rules beat `:host` unless `!important`; Vite doesn't watch `node_modules`; constructed stylesheets resolve `url()` against `baseURL`/the document; `@font-face` doesn't work in shadow roots, so moving fonts to the document is correct). Verdict: D89 is sound in direction; the plans overclaim in places, and some pieces are unowned. 44 findings (H1–H44). Status: **resolved** (C3 round 6): all group B recommendations accepted (recorded as D90, plus D85 revised); group A applied.
