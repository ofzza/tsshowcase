## Outcomes of group E (one by one)

- **E8 (2026-10-20):** committed as a work-in-progress snapshot (`b3be10e`) before any further change.
- **E1 (2026-10-20):** home wins: root documents (repo or package root) stay home-only unless listed explicitly in the merged config's `paths` (then also primary for the root); Z8's "incl. the root" applies only when the merged config sits below the package directory.
- **E3 (2026-10-20):** link resolution order: (1) nonexistent or ignored → broken; (2) a home doc, or a repo/package root directory → its home; (3) out of scope or outside every package → the VCS host; (4) candidates and selection.

Rule ids and defaults: `represent/missing-docs|tests|showcase` error; `tests/no-runner`, `tests/runner-failed` error; `config/several-configs`, `config/collection-clash`, `config/collection-membership`, `config/kind-conflict`, `config/same-base` error; `config/unused-key`, `config/missing-path`, `config/no-role`, `config/out-of-scope`, `config/shadowed`, `config/slug` warning; `docs/broken-link`, `docs/broken-anchor` warning (D123).
